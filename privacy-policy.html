Privacy Policy

5 Arch Brewing Co. — Mug Club Member Portal
Last updated: June 2026

5 Arch Brewing Co. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Mug Club Member Portal (the "Portal") at https://5archbrew.github.io/mug-club-portal/. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when creating or managing your Mug Club membership:

  • Name (first and last)
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address
  • Date of birth
  • Payment information (processed by QuickBooks Payments — we do not store payment card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Mug Club membership
  • Send login verification codes and membership-related communications
  • Generate and send invoices via QuickBooks Online
  • Notify you of upcoming member events
  • Administer autopay enrollment
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share your information only with:

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — to process invoices and payments. Subject to Intuit's Privacy Statement.
  • Google — the Portal is built on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. Your membership data is stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to authorized 5 Arch Brewing Co. staff. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • USPS — mailing addresses are submitted to the USPS Address Verification API for standardization. No data is retained by USPS beyond the verification request.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your membership data is stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to authorized 5 Arch Brewing Co. personnel. We use one-time login codes (no passwords) and HTTPS to protect data in transit. We do not store payment card information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your membership information for as long as your membership is active or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at jake@5archbrewing.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (available through the Portal)
  • Correct inaccurate information (available through the Portal's "Edit My Info" feature)
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jake@5archbrewing.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Portal is not directed at individuals under the age of 21. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active members of material changes by email. Continued use of the Portal after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
